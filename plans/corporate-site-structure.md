# Corporate site structure

Status: resolved for implementation on 2026-09-21. Self-review by Codex.

## Audience and meaning

Fortitudo needs to help an operating lead, founder or corporate buyer answer four questions without decoding agency language:

1. What can Fortitudo deliver?
2. Does the team understand our operating environment?
3. What real work can we inspect?
4. How will scope, review, ownership and support work?

The navigation therefore separates Services, Industries, Our Work and the engagement model. Copy stays specific about deliverables and boundaries. It does not imply certifications, regulatory advice, client outcomes or hiring plans that are not evidenced.

## Information architecture

- Services: delivery capabilities and individual service pages.
- Industries: an index plus Fintech, Ecommerce and Retail, Hospitality and Food, Logistics and Supply Chain, and Real Estate and PropTech.
- Our Work: factual public case studies.
- Blog: practical guidance and decision support.
- Company: About and Careers.
- Engagement: Approach, handover, ongoing support and contact.
- Language: access to machine translated browsing, with English identified as the source copy.

Each industry page is a front door. It names the buyer and context, the operating priorities, the delivery sequence, required inputs, scope boundaries, related services, relevant work and a next step.

## Visual plan

### Hierarchy

1. The industry or company promise is the first priority through the existing editorial hero.
2. Buyer fit and operating priorities are second through plain, ruled rows.
3. Delivery, scope, proof and contact follow in that order. Supporting labels remain quiet.

### Composition

The existing paid Imageworks hero, editorial sections, stacked delivery panels, scope notes, service lists and closing CTA remain the source components. New indexes use the existing pathway row structure. Navigation uses the supplied drawer and accordion structure. No new card system or decorative layer is introduced.

### Material

Existing background, border, muted surface and video treatments remain unchanged. Rows use spacing and rules to communicate grouping. No additional blur or panel treatment is needed.

### Details

The existing Geist sans roles, type scale, spacing, focus treatment, arrow links and rounded action controls remain unchanged. Expanded navigation stays keyboard reachable. External translation links name the translation provider and open in a new tab.

### Responsive behavior

Desktop keeps the current wide editorial columns. Mobile follows the existing single-column order, with the buyer question before its explanation. Drawer content remains vertically scrollable. Long industry and language names wrap rather than truncate. Reduced motion continues through the existing motion shell.

## Graphic decision

Preserve the existing HeroVideo and case study captures. Industry pages do not need new illustration because the content is operational and the real work links provide the useful visual evidence.

## Alternative considered

A single Industries page would be shorter, but it would force buyers to scan unrelated sector material and would not provide a focused search entry. Dedicated pages cost more copy maintenance but give each sector an honest, complete front door.

## Verification risks

- Expanded drawer height and focus order need desktop, short-height and mobile checks.
- Industry rows, delivery panels and related work need screenshot review at desktop and mobile widths.
- Google translated browsing is an external service and must remain labeled as machine translation.
