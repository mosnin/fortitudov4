# Fortitudo service pitch decks — research and design direction

Decision: rebuild all 14 downloadable service PDFs as concise, self-contained sales documents. Audience: business owners, executives and product/technical buyers evaluating an agency engagement. Research conducted 22 September 2026. Budget: six retained source bodies, targeted discovery plus challenge search; no paid crawl. These sources guide design judgment, not a tested claim that this layout increases conversions.

## What the evidence supports

1. **Design for reading without a presenter.** Duarte distinguishes visual leave-behinds from projected slides. Fortitudo PDFs need enough context to stand alone: who the service is for, the problem, what changes, what is delivered and what happens next. A cinematic headline alone is insufficient. [Duarte, 2019](https://www.duarte.com/blog/the-slides-you-deliver-versus-the-slidedoc-you-leave-behind/)
2. **Organize around the buyer's situation.** Duarte's sales-story guidance contrasts the customer's current problem with the situation a solution could enable. Translate this into specific friction, concrete delivery and qualified benefits. Do not turn possibilities into promised financial outcomes. [Duarte](https://www.duarte.com/blog/overcome-sales-objections-with-the-power-of-story/)
3. **Use takeaway headlines.** Harvard's communication guidance recommends a main finding in the title and one takeaway per slide. This is guidance for presented health communication, not agency-PDF outcome research; use the clarity principle while retaining reading context. [Harvard Chan](https://hsph.harvard.edu/research/health-communication/resources/strategies-for-clear-engaging-slides/)
4. **Let typography establish hierarchy.** Apple's typography session describes hierarchy through coordinated size, weight and leading. Adapt that care using Fortitudo's existing Geist family and visual identity. Apple UI guidance is not an Apple pitch-deck specification. [Apple WWDC20](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10175/)
5. **Remove noise, retain substance.** Nielsen Norman Group's minimalist-design guidance prioritizes necessary information over decoration. This supports the requested exclusion of decorative lines, icons and ornamental UI treatments. [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/aesthetic-minimalist-design/?lm=communication-not-decoration-ux-slogan-4&pt=youtubevideo)
6. **Restraint is not emptiness.** NN/g's qualitative desktop study found problems when related content was spread too far apart, while focused treatment sometimes helped complex material. This is website evidence, not a PDF experiment. The cautious transfer is to keep a benefit beside its explanation and avoid empty interstitial pages or repeated mood photography. [NN/g study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/content-dispersion/)

## Recommended deck architecture

This sequence is our synthesis, not a universal empirically proven template:

1. Branded cover: service name and a short, client-centered hook.
2. The problem: recognizable situations and their operational consequences.
3. The solution: how this service changes those situations; specific mechanism and deliverables.
4. Benefits of working with Fortitudo: connect each benefit to an actual working practice, such as one coordinated build, scoped decisions, verification or handover.
5. What the engagement includes: concise, concrete scope; retain service-specific distinctions.
6. Relevant work when genuinely supported by the current portfolio. Describe actual built functionality; omit performance claims without evidence. Do not use unrelated logos or invent testimonials.
7. How it works: the client's path from brief to scoped proposal, delivery reviews, verification and handover. Keep milestones together.
8. What to agree: inputs, scope drivers, third-party dependencies, acceptance and optional ongoing support. Detail may occupy a short appendix when needed.
9. Get started: what to send, where to send it, and what Fortitudo will do next. Use a real clickable enquiry URL and email.

Allow page count to follow the service. A specialist infrastructure offer needs different detail from a website offer. Do not force all 14 into identical paragraph lengths or fictional proof pages.

## Visual specification proposed for implementation

- Fortitudo name/mark and Geist only; restrained black, warm white and existing brand neutrals.
- Large sentence-case hook, strong headings, comfortable body leading. Avoid tiny explanatory/legal text.
- Use spacing, alignment and type scale to group information. No decorative rules, icons, card borders, connector arrows or fake dashboard motifs.
- A small family of deliberate compositions: cover photograph, large takeaway, paired pain/response, open editorial columns, compact process and closing action. Vary layout according to content, not randomly.
- Existing editorial photography can establish mood on cover/closing pages. It must not masquerade as client proof. No generated product brands or fictional software screenshots.
- Keep readable text off busy imagery unless a sufficiently opaque treatment provides reliable contrast.
- Build PDF text as selectable text, embed fonts, retain hyperlinks and descriptive document metadata. Check full-size pages and phone-width rendering, not contact sheets alone.

## Acceptance and limits

Every deck should answer the buyer's six questions: Is this for me? What problem does it address? What will I receive? Why this working arrangement? What must I contribute? How do I start? Check all 14 decks for coverage, truncation, overflow, whitespace, links and correct service classification.

No controlled study here establishes the best number of pages, exact type size, ideal photography ratio or conversion effect for Fortitudo's buyers. Those are design decisions to validate with real prospects. No source verifies Fortitudo's operational claims; repository offer records and real project evidence remain the authority. Reader testing or factual changes could invalidate our specific layout/copy choices. Six bodies span four publishing organizations; the two Duarte pieces and two NN/g pieces are grouped, not counted as independent replications.

The retained sources are the complete representations returned by the web tool, not a claim of full original HTML/video/PDF capture. HIG direct rendering failed (JavaScript shell), so the full WWDC transcript is used instead. The original assertion-evidence tutorial moved; its old search snippet is not used as evidence. Search receipts include screened-out alternatives. Independent review status is recorded separately in the dossier/review artifacts.
