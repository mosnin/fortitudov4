# Persuasion brief

Audience: a business buyer comparing a scoped website, ecommerce, software, AI or advisory engagement. Desired decision: understand fit and choose whether to send a brief. Contact starts a conversation; the written proposal confirms commercial terms.

Use authority only through inspectable projects and concrete acceptance checks. Use commitment through a small, relevant first step the buyer can choose. Use liking through plain, respectful language about the buyer’s work. Do not manufacture social proof, scarcity, urgency, reciprocal obligation or enterprise-client credentials.

The alternative in each deck makes poor fit visible. Readers may inspect the work, request a smaller engagement, use another provider or take no action. There is no checkout, deadline, gating of essential information or automatic commitment.

The service descriptions are declared scope, not independent evidence that every historical delivery achieved those results. Project-specific access, permissions and third-party availability remain qualified in the catalog boundaries. Independent review accompanies this revision.
