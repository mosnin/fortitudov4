# Proposed comprehension check — not yet run

Uncertainty: whether a prospective buyer can identify the right service and distinguish the offered scope from guaranteed commercial outcomes.

Segment: buyers currently considering one of the 14 service categories. Ask them to read the relevant PDF, then describe the result, required inputs, scope limits, first review point, next step and whether contact commits them to a purchase.

Primary observation: accurate explanation of those six items without prompting. Record confusion and exact wording; do not substitute click rate for comprehension. Guardrails: perceived pressure, mistaken fixed-price or speed promises, mistaken guaranteed performance, and missing service scope.

Decision rule: revise any wording that causes a material misunderstanding before extending the test. Do not claim conversion improvement without a separately designed comparison and adequate evidence. No outreach or experiment was executed in this copy task.
