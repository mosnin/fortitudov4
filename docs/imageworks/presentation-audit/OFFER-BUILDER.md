# Presentation improvement worksheet

Mode: improve presentation, audit commercial structure. Owner: Fortitudo. Sources: current catalog, offer data and route components; no authenticated Company OS or Symbolic context tools were available in this session. No companion pricing, funnel or influence invocation is claimed. Existing terms are preserved.

## Decisions
- Keep 14 services and their specialist distinctions. A narrower invented niche would discard authorized scope.
- Use open deliverable lists, modest type and thin rules. Remove decorative numbering where order has no meaning; keep numbering for actual delivery steps.
- Move relevant project evidence immediately after the concise engagement on core service pages. Buyers should see what the agency has built before reading all implementation detail.
- On specialist pages, explain the offer and who it serves before the workflow strip. Preserve the specialist implementation sections.
- Retain detailed scope, inputs, exclusions, acceptance and PDF access. Cutting those would simplify appearance at the expense of informed purchasing.
- Remove rounded FAQ cards, nested pricing terms cards and animated featured-price borders. Preserve FAQ interaction, video heroes, source motion, CTA behavior and the requested footer.

## Value diagnosis
Desired outcome: a working business asset; retained explicit deliverables. Likelihood: inspectable projects and acceptance criteria, not invented ROI. Delay: actual schedule remains a proposal dependency, no fixed turnaround invented. Effort: inputs and review obligations stay visible; handover reduces dependence, but training effectiveness is unmeasured.

## Obstacle journey
Before purchase: unclear fit -> audience and fit copy; uncertain scope -> consultation or written proposal; proof -> work links. During delivery: missing access/content -> inputs and milestone reviews; vendor limits -> explicit eligibility and usage exclusions; scope change -> separately agreed work. After launch: operating the asset -> handover and documentation; future maintenance -> optional support with agreed boundaries. Actual staffing, unit cost and stressed capacity remain unknown.

## Scenario review
Late inputs require a revised agreed schedule, not an unchanged launch promise. Low adoption is not solved by promising revenue: review usability against agreed journeys. Support overload requires a defined support scope, not unlimited work. A failed acceptance scenario remains unfinished work under the agreement; the marketing page does not invent a refund remedy. No additional performance guarantee or scarcity is offered. A declined buyer can download the PDF and leave without submitting a form.

## Page structure review
Home: introduction -> selected work -> four service families -> selected decks -> enquiry. Service directory: select a relevant service, no invented tiers. Core services: outcome -> fit/engagement/deliverables -> relevant work -> detailed scope/options -> process/acceptance/terms -> PDF -> objections -> enquiry. Specialists: outcome -> engagement/deliverables -> implementation flow -> use cases -> delivery/acceptance/boundaries -> PDF -> FAQ -> scoped enquiry. Resources: ungated document access and matching service detail. Work: direct project inspection, categories and external links. About: founder, principles, process and relevant services. Pricing: consultation, scoped build or optional ongoing work; no unsupported prices. Contact: selected engagement, brief, explanation of next step and email fallback. Supporting solution, approach, handover and support routes retain their distinct educational roles.

## Outstanding evidence, not presentation defects
No measured conversion baseline, customer interviews, cost/capacity model or authenticated provider-context packet was retrieved. Existing founder impression/experience claims are owner-supplied, not independently verified. Specific payment and renewal terms require each actual proposal. These are not silently marked passing because the website builds.

## Next experiment (proposed, not executed)
Five business owners/product leads with an actual project review their relevant service page and PDF. Ask them to explain the result, exclusions, their inputs and next step without prompting. Proceed if at least four correctly identify all four; revise any wording that causes a false guarantee or ownership expectation. No sales-pressure or price-change experiment. Stop if any participant interprets editorial imagery as client-result proof. A later paid pilot must measure delivery effort and margin before capacity claims are made.
