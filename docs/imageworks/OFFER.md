# Fortitudo offer — revision 2

Status: implemented public-site proposal; economics and buyer response unvalidated. Context: user request, existing production website and source at baseline.json. No CompanyOS or Symbolic provider context was connected.

For business owners and product leads planning a website, online store, software product or AI workflow. Choose a consultation when the scope is uncertain, a fixed-scope build when the requirements can be agreed, or ongoing improvements for an existing product. Procurement approval and project ownership remain with the buyer.

## Plan the project
A defined review of the current site, product or workflow. Compare practical options, identify dependencies and risks, and receive a written plan. Scope and price are agreed before the review begins. No free comprehensive audit or fixed turnaround is promised.

## Build and launch
A written scope, design and development, agreed integrations, review milestones, testing, launch and handover. The proposal defines deliverables, timeline, revision rounds and fixed project price. Changes outside the agreed scope are priced separately. A live working deliverable is the intended result; increased revenue is not guaranteed.

## Improve what is live
A monthly scope for agreed improvements, maintenance and monitoring. Priorities, response expectations, term, cancellation and renewal require the actual proposal. The website does not advertise unlimited work or 24-hour support.

## Responsibilities and exclusions
The buyer supplies or approves content, access and feedback. Third-party subscriptions, licenses and ongoing support are identified separately. Ownership transfers on full payment under existing terms. No unverified price anchor, discount, capacity limit, countdown, bonus stack or performance guarantee. Poor fit: buyers requiring guaranteed sales, unlimited scope or an immediate launch without agreed inputs.

## Evidence and decision
Eight published cases remain the proof of work: Stored, Chippi, NeverAge, Two Cookies NYC, Nourish Reserve, Hannah Joy, Govern and Tellme. The project descriptions are not measured ROI evidence or client testimonials. Request a proposal, explore the work or leave without submitting anything. Contact collection uses the existing leads endpoint and privacy policy.
