## Findings

No material visual blocker remains in the reviewed final samples.

The consolidated three-column scope pages retain clear heading-to-body relationships, comfortable line spacing and sufficient bottom clearance. The densest inspected page, AI planning, keeps the closing proposal paragraph above the footer with a visible gap. No clipped paragraphs, overlapping columns or decorative rules/icons were observed. The Fortitudo mark functions as branding. Photography stays separate from the reading areas and is not presented as project evidence.

The earlier fragmentation issue is addressed: websites now combines publishing, connected journeys and launch; ecommerce keeps migration and platform eligibility together; planning and responsibilities share one page. The second options page now uses “Extend or improve what you have,” avoiding an identical consecutive headline.

Hooks and pains vary by service. The sampled benefits are supported by the existing offer records: websites connects copy, design, CMS and handover; ecommerce connects catalog, integrations, test orders and operation; software combines customer and owner workflows with an optional-support handover; AI specifies context, permissions, evaluation and operating controls. These are proposed delivery practices, not evidence of realized customer outcomes. No invented statistics, testimonials, revenue guarantees or unrestricted AI autonomy promises were found in the reviewed narrative content.

The common structure and black reading surfaces make the collection consistent. The differing cover photography and service-specific language provide useful distinction without adding ornaments. Detailed scope remains available after the initial hook, challenge, solution, benefits and process.

## Limits

This review does not independently verify automated PDF bounds, embedded fonts, hyperlinks, deployment, or all 14 decks' factual delivery claims. It does not establish reader comprehension or conversion performance. Landscape PDFs still require zoom or landscape viewing on small phones; these are not reflowable mobile documents. No claim of Apple endorsement or Apple-equivalent design acceptance is made.

The separate research review remains unchanged: six narrow claims supported, question coverage sufficient for design guidance, discovery limited, lineage adequate. No kernel admission is inferred from these visual findings.
